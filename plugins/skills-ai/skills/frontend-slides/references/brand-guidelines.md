## Identity
- Brand/product: (unset — per project)
- Personality, 3 adjectives: precise, calm, technical (default)

## Color
- Mode: dark-first, light acceptable (default)
- Background: `#0F1115` dark / `#FAFAF7` light (default)
- Surface: `#171A21` dark / `#FFFFFF` light (default)
- Text: `#E8EAED` dark / `#1A1C1E` light (default)
- Muted text: `#9AA0A8` (default)
- Primary accent: `#4F7CFF` (default)
- Success `#3FB68B` · Warning `#E5A53D` · Error `#E5564F` (default)
- Rule: one accent per view for primary action; never more than two accents total.

## Typography
- Display: Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif (default)
- Body: Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif (default)
- Mono: "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, monospace (default)
- Scale (px): 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 28 / 40 / 56 (default)
- Headings: 600–700 weight, tight letter-spacing (-0.01em to -0.03em at display sizes) (default)

## Spacing & layout
- Base unit 4px; steps: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 (default)
- Max content width: 1100px; reading width: 68ch (default)
- Border radius: 8px standard, 12px cards, 999px pills (default)
- Shadows: subtle, single-layer; prefer borders (`1px solid` at low alpha) over heavy shadows (default)

## Components
- Buttons: solid accent for primary, quiet outline for secondary; no more than one primary per view (default)
- Cards: surface color + hairline border; hover lift optional and subtle (default)
- Forms: labels above inputs, generous hit targets (≥40px), inline validation (default)

## Voice
- Tone: direct, concrete, no marketing superlatives (default)
- Avoid: exclamation marks, "unlock/supercharge/seamless", filler taglines (default)

## Hard avoids
- Purple-gradient-on-white "AI slop" look
- Emoji as icons
- Lorem ipsum in any deliverable
- Carousels (default — remove if you like them)
